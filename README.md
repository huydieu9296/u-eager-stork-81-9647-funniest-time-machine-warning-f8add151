## Entry

> WARNING: Do not meet yourself. Do not marry yourself. Do not invest in your own startup. You will regret all three.

The joke escalates through three forbidden time-travel acts — meeting, marrying, and funding yourself — each worse than the last, and lands on the punchline that you would regret all three. Short, sharp, and exactly 115 characters, just under the 120-character limit.

Why it works: the first warning sounds like standard time-travel advice, the second turns it personal, and the third turns it financial — the classic rule of three, with the last item (your own startup) being the one every founder secretly fears.
